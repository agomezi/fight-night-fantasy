Fight Night Fantasy

Privacy Policy

Effective October 4, 2026

Fight Night Fantasy is a free fantasy game for UFC events. This policy explains what the app collects, why, who processes it, and how to delete it. We keep it short because we collect very little.

What we collect

  • Sign-in details. When you sign in with Apple or Google we receive an account identifier and the email address that provider shares with us. If you use Apple's "Hide My Email", we only see the relay address.
  • Your display name, and the names you have used before. We keep past names so that offensive names can be moderated and the weekly name-change limit can be enforced.
  • Game activity: your picks, the points they score, your leaderboard position, and any leagues you create or join.
  • Name reports. If you report another player's name, we store who made the report, which name was reported, and any reason you gave.
  • Notification settings and a push token. If you allow notifications, we store your device's push token, its platform (iOS or Android), and which notifications you have turned on.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifier. We do not use analytics or advertising SDKs, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To run the game: lock and score picks, and build leaderboards and league standings.
  • To show your display name to other players on leaderboards and in leagues.
  • To send the notifications you have turned on, such as pick-lock reminders and results.
  • To moderate display names.

We never sell your data and never use it for advertising.

Who processes it

We use a small number of service providers, only to run the app:

  • Supabase hosts our database and handles sign-in. Data is stored in the United States.
  • Apple and Google handle sign-in when you choose them.
  • Expo delivers push notifications and app updates. Apple and Google then carry the notifications to your device.

What other players see

Your display name, points, accuracy, and leaderboard or league position are visible to other players. Your email address and sign-in details are never shown to anyone.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the app under Settings → Delete Account. When you do:

  • Your sign-in details, email address, current and past display names, push tokens, and notification settings are erased immediately.
  • You are removed from the global leaderboard immediately, and picks for events that have not started are deleted.
  • To avoid rewriting a league's standings mid-season, your scored results stay in any league you were in until the current season ends (at most about three months). They are shown as "Former Member" and cannot be linked back to you. They are permanently deleted when the next season begins.

Retention

We keep your data for as long as your account exists, and delete it as described above when you delete your account.

Children

Fight Night Fantasy is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

You can see and change your display name and notification settings in the app, and delete your account at any time. To request a copy of your data, or for any other privacy question, contact us at the address below.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and, for significant changes, let you know in the app.
